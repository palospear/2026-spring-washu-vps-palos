I long for the happiness of playing with my friends and the fearlessness I once felt when expressing myself. During elementary school and the beginning of junior high, I was good at organizing friends to play together and sharing joy with them. But later, I found myself doing worse academically than others and having a hard time getting involved with new groups after COVID-19. To be honest, no one treats me as their best friend, even though I try to be kind. I often worry about whether I am doing things right or if I am just moving myself with my own efforts. I miss the qualities I used to have that helped me socialize well and make others feel comfortable.

It is actually hard to draw a profound conclusion from this, as my desire for connection might simply be caused by the harsh environment and my lack of friends at the time. One point is that humans are social animals; therefore, we need to be recognized and accepted by others. Besides, positive feedback is significant because it gives individuals courage when interacting with the world. On one hand, thinking deeply about everything and getting used to self-reflection interferes with expressing my true self to the outside world. On the other hand, as long as I am honest and make myself comfortable, it is absolutely acceptable to embrace different lifestyles.
