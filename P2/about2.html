For countless centuries, the I Ching has served as a timeless guide to understanding the cosmic vibrations of the human experience.

STEP 01

The Question

Focus your mind. Avoid "yes" or "no" questions. Instead, ask "What if..." or "What is this situation all about?" This allows the oracle to provide a commentary-style insight.

STEP 02

Casting Coins

Using three coins, the randomness reflects your current energetic state. Six casts build a hexagram from the bottom up, creating a map of your situation.

STEP 03

Interpreting

The hexagram you create is a mirror. Apply its wisdom introspectively. Your intuition will confirm if you have been enlightened by the truth.
